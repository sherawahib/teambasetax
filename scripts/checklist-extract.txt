
Tax Client Checklist
Please use this checklist to gather the documents and information needed to prepare your tax return.
Personal Information
[ ]Full names of taxpayer, spouse, and dependents
[ ]Current mailing address
[ ]Social Security numbers for all applicable individuals
[ ]Dates of birth for all applicable individuals
[ ]Copy of driver’s license or state ID
[ ]Email address and phone number
[ ]Mortgage statement if you own a home
[ ]College tuition statements and education expense records
Income Documents
[ ]W-2 forms
[ ]1099-R forms for retirement income
[ ]1099-MISC forms
[ ]1099-NEC forms
[ ]1099-K forms
[ ]Social Security income statement
[ ]Year-end retirement account statements
[ ]Disability income documents, if applicable
Business Information
[ ]Business name and address
[ ]Employer Identification Number (EIN)
[ ]Principal business activity
[ ]Business income from all sources
[ ]Twelve months of business bank statements
[ ]Official financial statement or profit and loss statement
[ ]Copy of prior-year business tax return
[ ]1099-MISC, 1099-NEC, and 1099-K forms received
Vehicle Expenses for Business Use
[ ]Vehicle make, model, and year
[ ]Purchase date, purchase amount, and purchase contract
[ ]Auto insurance records
[ ]Total annual miles driven
[ ]Total business miles driven
[ ]Vehicle repairs and maintenance
[ ]Vehicle registration date and registration cost
Other Business Expenses
[ ]Advertising
[ ]Commissions and fees
[ ]Contract labor, including 1099-MISC or 1099-NEC forms issued to contractors
[ ]Legal and professional fees, including attorney, accountant, bookkeeper, and tax preparer fees
[ ]Rent
[ ]Supplies and materials
[ ]Travel and meals
[ ]Utilities
[ ]Health insurance
[ ]Equipment purchased for business during the tax year
Client Notes
Please provide any additional information that may affect your tax return, including major changes, new dependents, address changes, business changes, or unusual income or expenses.
